QuantumData IT Solutions – FZCO is an information technology company licensed in the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones. We work on data classification and analysis, IT infrastructure, and technology consulting for clients who need their reporting and their systems to agree with each other.

What we do

  • Data classification & analysis

    Structuring, classifying and analysing operational and financial data so it can be reported on and relied upon.

  • IT infrastructure

    Design and setup of the data platforms, storage and integration layers that carry that data.

  • Information technology consulting

    Advice on tooling, architecture and the practical steps between the current system and the intended one.

  • Science & technology consultancy

    Method and feasibility work ahead of implementation, including analytical model design.

  • Computers & peripheral equipment trading

    Supply of hardware where a project requires it.
